HookJump Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

HookJump is a browser extension that learns which part of a song listeners enjoy most (the "hook") on YouTube and YouTube Music, and offers a button to jump to it.

What we collect

  • Video ID of the YouTube video being played (the public 11-character id in the URL).
  • Listening signals per 5-second segment: numeric rewards such as "listened", "skipped", "replayed", or "fire button pressed".
  • A random install ID generated by the extension, used only to rate-limit submissions.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, Google account, IP-based profile, browsing history, search history, or comments.
  • No data from any website other than youtube.com and music.youtube.com.
  • Signals are added into per-segment totals; individual listening sessions are not stored.

How it is used

Totals are used only to compute which part of each song is most enjoyed, so every HookJump user gets better "jump to hook" suggestions. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your personal learning stays in your browser's local extension storage. Shared totals are stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database that can only be accessed through validated functions.

Your choices

  • Turn off Community learning in the extension popup to stop sending any data.
  • Turn off Learning on to pause the extension completely.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all local data.
